The Origin

I didn't come from a traditional tech background. I came from curiosity, necessity, and a refusal to stay stuck. I taught myself to code because I had problems I needed to solve — and once I started, I couldn't stop.

Over time, one skill turned into many. Web development led to AI. IT led to cybersecurity. None of it was planned. All of it was intentional.

Today I build technology for real organizations, real clients, and real people — websites that perform, tools that work, and systems that last. Based in Chillicothe, serving businesses across Central Ohio, throughout Ohio, and remotely.
