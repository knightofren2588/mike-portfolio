Case Study

Showcase Transport LLC

A custom website built from the ground up for a freight and transport company that had no online presence before this project.

🚚 Showcase Transport LLC 📅 Launched Nov 11, 2025 💼 Paid Client Project
🌐 View Live Site

🎯 The Challenge

Showcase Transport LLC didn't have a website before this project. Potential customers had no way to learn about the company online, understand what services they offered, or reach out with an inquiry.

🧭 The Goals

  • ✓ Establish a professional online presence
  • ✓ Explain their services clearly
  • ✓ Build credibility
  • ✓ Make the company easier to find online
  • ✓ Showcase the business visually
  • ✓ Give potential customers a way to submit inquiries

💡 The Solution

I designed and built a custom website from scratch — fast, responsive, and visually distinct, with a custom truck animation that reflects the business and a direct inquiry path built in from day one.

🛠️ What I Built

  • ✓ Custom-designed, fully responsive website
  • ✓ Custom truck animation
  • ✓ Progressive Web App (PWA) functionality
  • ✓ Integrated JotForm inquiry system
  • ✓ Mobile-first, fast-loading pages

⚙️ Technology

React Vite Tailwind CSS Netlify PWA JotForm

🚀 Launch & Result

Showcase Transport went from having no dedicated website to having a professional, responsive online presence where potential customers can learn about the company, understand its services, and submit inquiries. The site launched November 11, 2025.
